A Slogan for ROCAMA

Our Story

Born of Wonder
Rooted in Legacy

1937
Grandpa founded RACOMA

They say, at the very beginning
there wasn’t even a name.

I only remember Grandpa from my childhood,
always full of excitement,
always making a new RACOMA for us to play with.

Grandpa was a man of few words.
What he made? was it furniture, or was it a toy?

Every day, he kept making.
Furniture or toy, it didn’t really matter.

It just had to be fun.
